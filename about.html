Building the connective layer for Indian golf.

ForeSports Private Limited is an early-stage company based in Delhi NCR, building Fore — a platform that links golf facilities, coaches and golfers on one system.

Why we're building this.

Golf in India runs on phone calls, WhatsApp messages and paper registers. Courses and ranges routinely have tee times and practice slots that go unsold. Coaches build their reputation one referral at a time. And a golfer's own history — scores, lessons, handicap — lives nowhere in particular.

We started ForeSports to build the system that's missing: one platform that gives facilities a reason to adopt software (because it makes them money, not costs them money), gives coaches a storefront independent of any one course, and gives golfers a single place to find and book any part of the game.

Where we are.

We're pre-product — currently building the platform and setting up the business, starting with facility partnerships in Delhi NCR. We plan to raise funding after launch and initial traction, not before, because we'd rather show the model working than describe it.

We're a small team right now. We'll share more about who's building this as that team grows.
