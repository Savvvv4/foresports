How FORE works

Start with the facility. Connect the whole game.

The strategy is intentionally sequenced: build the operating layer first, then open discovery and coaching, then connect the ecosystem around the golfer.

01 · NOW

Facilities

Deploy the Facility OS around inventory, bookings, utilization and revenue opportunities.

02 · NEXT

Coaches + golfers

Use facility relationships to introduce discovery, lessons, practice and player records.

03 · THEN

Connected golf

Let demand, relationships and data compound across the network.

One connected loop

Golfers ↔ Coaches ↔ Facilities

Each side creates value for the others. The objective is not three isolated products; it is one connected system.

Facilities create accessInventory becomes discoverable, bookable and easier to manage.
Coaches create improvementGolfers find expertise and connect lessons to practice and progress.
Golfers create demandMore participation gives facilities and coaches a stronger digital market.
The long-term flywheel

The more connected the network becomes, the more useful it gets.

More facilities

More places to discover and more inventory to make available.

More golfers

More demand and more opportunities for facilities and coaches.

More coaches

More choice and better pathways for golfers looking to improve.

More data

A richer picture of the golfer journey across play, practice and learning.

The vision

Golf, connected from the ground up.

FORE is building toward a digital infrastructure layer for the golf ecosystem, starting in India.
