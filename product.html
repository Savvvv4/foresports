The platform

One operating layer. Three sides of the golf economy.

Fore is being built as a B2B2C platform connecting venues, coaches and golfers around the same inventory, booking and customer data.

01

For facilities

Turn underutilized inventory into incremental revenue without adding an upfront software cost.

  • Dynamic pricing for off-peak tee times and practice slots
  • Marketplace demand and customer acquisition
  • Utilization visibility and operational analytics
  • Paths from first booking to passes and memberships
  • Cross-sell opportunities across pro shop and F&B
02

For coaches

Give coaches a digital storefront and the tools to turn interest into repeat instruction.

  • Coach profile and discovery
  • Scheduling and trial-session workflows
  • Lesson packages and recurring offerings
  • Performance and lesson history
03

For golfers

A single place to discover, book and build continuity across playing, practicing and learning.

  • Course, range and coach discovery
  • Tee-time and lesson booking
  • Packages and memberships
  • Persistent performance and lesson history

The loop

Every transaction strengthens the network.

Facilities provide inventory. Fore makes that inventory discoverable and bookable. Golfers create demand. Coaches add learning and retention. The shared data layer improves the next interaction.
