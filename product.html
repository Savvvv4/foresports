Partner your facility

One system, three sides.

Fore connects golf facilities, coaches and golfers on a shared booking and data layer — so inventory that used to go unsold gets filled, and a golfer's history follows them wherever they play.

How the model works.

1

A facility deploys Fore at no cost.

No software fee, no setup cost. Fore replaces phone-and-notebook booking with one system for tee times, range slots, carts and caddies.

2

Fore fills the inventory that would otherwise go empty.

Off-peak tee times and range bays are priced dynamically and sold through the Fore app — the same logic hotels and airlines use for perishable inventory, applied to golf for the first time in India.

3

Everyone gets something they didn't have before.

The facility earns new revenue and gains a customer-acquisition channel. Golfers get access to inventory they couldn't previously find. Fore takes a share of that new, previously-unsold inventory — instead of charging for the software itself.

What each side of Fore does.

Facilities

Dynamic pricing on tee times and range slots, a maintenance calendar that blocks availability live, utilization analytics, and member management — replacing desks, notebooks and phone calls with one system.

Coaches

A profile with fees, qualifications and packages, a calendar for lessons and trials, and reviews that build discoverability over time — independent of any one facility.

Golfers

Discover courses, ranges and coaches nearby, book tee times, carts, caddies and lessons in one flow, buy packages and memberships, and keep a scorecard and handicap record that's yours, not the facility's.

Where India's golf market stands.

India has crossed 400 golf courses, with driving ranges and academies expanding alongside them. Golf equipment sales are growing at roughly 25% a year, and India's indoor golf and simulator segment — currently upward of $40M — sits inside a global category headed toward several billion dollars by the early 2030s.

Globally, models like this already exist: GolfNow runs the world's largest tee-time marketplace using dynamic pricing; Lightspeed, Teesnap and CoachNow serve similar functions for courses and coaches. None operate in India. The closest India-specific product, 4moles, has built a large directory of registered golfers but functions as a listings and community platform rather than a revenue or booking system.

What's different about Fore

  • Dynamic pricing for perishable golf inventory — new to the Indian market.
  • Zero-cost adoption for facilities, since Fore is paid from new inventory sold, not a software fee.
  • One continuous record across facility, coach and golfer — nobody currently connects all three.
