The commercial model

Software aligned with incremental revenue.

The proposed facility model is designed to reduce the upfront barrier to adoption.

01

No upfront software fee

Fore is designed around an inventory-for-software model rather than requiring a traditional SaaS subscription at the outset.

02

Work the dead inventory

Fore can use selected low-demand inventory as the economic basis for deployment and distribution.

03

Grow into infrastructure

As golfer demand and coaching supply build, the facility relationship becomes more valuable than a simple booking widget.
