What Fore gives you.

  • A profile that sells for you. Fees, qualifications, packages, availability and specialties, visible to golfers actively looking for a coach.
  • A calendar that fills itself. Lesson bookings and trial sessions land directly on your schedule.
  • Package and trial-lesson tools. Offer discovery lessons and multi-lesson bundles without building your own payment or booking flow.
  • Reputation that compounds. Reviews and ratings build your discoverability over time, and follow you rather than the facility you happen to be coaching at.
  • Payments handled for you. Get paid directly through the platform, with receipts and invoicing built in.

Fore is still in development. We're building the coach experience alongside the facility partnerships that will bring golfers into the platform. Joining the waitlist means you'll be among the first coaches we bring on when it's ready.
