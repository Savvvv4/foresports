The problem

Golf in India is growing. Its operating infrastructure is fragmented.

Courses and ranges still rely heavily on calls, WhatsApp, manual scheduling and static pricing. Customer relationships are split across facilities, coaches and disconnected systems.

01

Perishable inventory

An empty tee time or practice slot expires without generating revenue.

02

Manual operations

Bookings, cancellations, passes and customer requests create avoidable operational load.

03

Disconnected customers

There is no shared layer connecting where a golfer plays, practices, learns and spends.

Why now

A large market is ready for a better operating layer.

India's golf ecosystem is expanding across traditional courses, driving ranges, academies and emerging indoor formats. The opportunity is not simply another listing product — it is the infrastructure connecting supply and demand.

Our thesis

Golf inventory behaves more like hospitality inventory than a traditional software asset.

Demand varies by day, time and customer. Fore is being built to help facilities capture more of that demand without adding another fixed software cost.

The model

Software that earns when the facility earns.

Fore's initial facility model is designed around inventory-for-software, rather than a conventional upfront SaaS fee.
