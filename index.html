Why now.

Golf in India is growing quickly, but the infrastructure hasn't caught up. Bookings still happen over the phone and on WhatsApp. Empty tee times and range bays go unsold. Coaches rely entirely on word of mouth. Nothing connects a golfer's history across the courses, ranges and coaches they actually use.

Fore is built to be that connective layer — starting in Delhi NCR.

400+ golf courses across India
~25% year-on-year growth in golf equipment sales
$40M+ India's indoor golf & simulator segment, and growing

How the model works.

Fore doesn't charge facilities for its software. Instead, in exchange for deploying the platform, Fore takes a share of previously unbooked tee-time and range inventory to sell directly — so a facility's cost to adopt is zero.

Read how it works

This is the same idea behind dynamic pricing in hotels and airlines, applied to a category — perishable golf inventory — where it hasn't existed in India before.
